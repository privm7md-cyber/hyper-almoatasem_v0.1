## 1. Purpose and scope

Strategic foundation guiding every design decision for the Hyper Al-Moatasem online hypermarket.
Usable as source of truth by designers and AI coding agents. Non-specialist Arabic is authoritative;
English strategy terms kept where standard.

## 2. Existing decisions and constraints

- Backend complete (incl. true account-less guest orders); pricing/inventory/availability/promotion
  authorities live in backend — frontend displays, never invents.
- Personality direction (§5 PO contract): trustworthy, local, modern, practical, simple —
  "respectable local supermarket"; not luxury, not chaotic-discount (LOCKED, refined herein).
- Arabic-first, RTL, EGP (`152 ج.م` style), Africa/Cairo, Gregorian (LOCKED).
- No brand assets exist (no logo; public/ holds only framework defaults) — identity work pending.
- No customer research or competitor study exists — superiority claims are hypotheses, never facts.

## 3. Approved brand positioning — Modern Local Hypermarket (refined, LOCKED)

Retain the candidate direction with binding refinements: modern = organized digital fluency
(usability, clarity, consistency), never tech-company coldness; local = warmth and familiarity,
never outdated/busy traditionalism; quality shows in execution and coherence, never luxury cues.

## 7. Primary traits (LOCKED; supporting: modern)

1. **موثوق** — trust via clear prices/info/accuracy. Behavior: honest weights, availability, drift surfacing. Not: claimed superiority. Test: can the customer verify this claim from the screen alone?
2. **قريب ودافئ** — simple human familiar language. Not: slang overload or fake friendship. Test: would a neighbor say this naturally?
3. **منظم وواضح** — browsing/understanding/ordering stays easy and direct. Not: decoration over information. Test: does every element earn its pixels for the task?
4. **عملي وعادل القيمة** — daily needs, understandable prices, real offers only. Not: cheap-looking or promo-shouting. Test: is any discount shown backed by real data?
5. **هادئ وواثق** — visually comfortable; expression only with purpose. Not: urgent or dull. Test: does anything compete for attention without customer value?

## 8. Brand promise (LOCKED)

**«احتياجات بيتك بسهولة ووضوح، وثقة في كل خطوة.»**
Scope note: clarity/information trust, not service guarantees (delivery times need future validation); never cheapest-claim; never assortment-completeness claim (stock-dependent).

## 9. Differentiation strategy (LOCKED)

- **Primary: commercial transparency** — clear price/unit, honest weights with actuals explained, visible availability, non-misleading offers, order/replacement status clarity. Problem solved: "do I understand what I buy, at what price, and what happens next?" Credibility condition: backend already enforces it (snapshots, actuals, drift surfacing, OOS visibility) — frontend must not dilute it. Hard to copy via skin: requires operational honesty across catalog, pricing, and fulfillment communication, not colors.
- **Supporting 1: household-ease organization** — big catalog made navigable; transparency made practical.
- **Supporting 2: trusted local familiarity** — tone and proximity.
- Explicitly NOT claimed: market-wide superiority (no evidence; would need validation).

## 10. First-five-seconds emotional goal (LOCKED)

Primary: **trust/reassurance through clarity and organization** ("organized, respectable place that understands daily needs"). Supporters: comfort/ease (immediately after), organized variety (never overclaimed). Avoid: luxury awe, promo noise, urgency/anxiety, boredom.

## 11. Five-Second Brand Test (LOCKED, evaluative aid — not scientific proof)

1. ده متجر إيه؟ (واضح إنه هايبر للتسوق اليومي؟) 2. لمين؟ (مناسب لاحتياجاتي والسوق المحلي؟)
3. الأسعار والمعلومات مفهومة؟ (سعر/وحدة/عروض بلا غموض؟) 4. أعرف أبدأ منين؟ (أقسام/بحث واضحة؟)
5. ثقة أم ضغط؟ (منظم ومطمئن أم رسائل متنافسة؟)

## 12. Design No-Gos (LOCKED, prioritized)

1. No unsupported claims (cheapest/fastest/best without proof). Detect: does any copy assert leadership? 2. No promo noise (every screen an ad). Detect: count competing promotional elements per viewport. 3. No ambiguous prices (price without unit/weight clarity). Detect: can price+unit be read in one glance? 4. No cheap look (shouting discounts, fake counters). Detect: would this embarrass next to the real price? 5. No anonymous template look. Detect: could the logo be swapped with any supermarket? 6. No alienating luxury / cold corporate tone. Detect: does it feel daily-shoppable and human? 7. No sloppy Arabic or misleading product/unit wording. Detect: terminology review, esp. weights/units. 8. No decoration competing with product information. Detect: does removing it hurt the task? Creative freedom remains in illustration style, tone within traits, and calm promotional layouts.

## 13. Coherence audit (LOCKED, accepted)

No open contradictions: simplicity↔promotion resolved by purpose-gated expression; value↔exclusivity by luxury ban; professionalism↔pricing by transparency mandate; familiarity↔tone by warm-native rule; speed↔pressure by operation/feeling split; promise scope limited to information honesty; playfulness fenced off money contexts. Register accepted by owner.
